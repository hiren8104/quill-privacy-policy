Privacy Policy

Quill – Money Manager & SMS Expense Tracker

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quill ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Quill mobile application ("App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and financial information. Please read this policy carefully before using the App.

1. Information We Access and Process

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Quill may access or process the following information:

  • SMS messages: With your permission, Quill may access incoming SMS messages to identify bank alerts, payment notifications, transaction amounts, dates, and other relevant transaction details.
  • Financial transaction information: Transaction amounts, descriptions, dates, payment methods, account or bank labels, categories, and other information that you add or that the App extracts from supported transaction notifications.
  • Information you provide: Any information you manually enter into the App, such as expenses, income, categories, budgets, and notes.
  • Device and technical information: Certain technical information may be processed by the App and its integrated service providers, including device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app version, and diagnostic information.
  • Notification information: If you enable notifications, the App may use notification permissions to display transaction alerts and other app notifications.

2. How We Use SMS Permissions

Quill requests SMS permissions to provide its SMS-based expense tracking and transaction detection features.

SMS access is used to identify relevant financial notifications and convert supported transaction messages into expense or income records.

Important: SMS messages and transaction details are intended to be processed on your device for the App's transaction-tracking functionality. Quill does not use SMS content for advertising or sell SMS message contents to third parties.

You can control SMS permissions through your Android device settings. If you deny or revoke SMS permissions, features that depend on SMS access may not work.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed or provided through Quill may be used to:

  • Identify and organize supported financial transactions.
  • Display income, expenses, balances, and spending summaries.
  • Generate reports, charts, budgets, and financial insights.
  • Provide transaction alerts and app notifications.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
  • Display advertisements through advertising partners.
  • Protect the App and help prevent misuse or technical issues.

4. Data Storage and Security

Financial records and transaction information processed by Quill are intended to be stored locally on your device for the App's core functionality.

We do not operate a service that sells your personal financial transaction records. We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App and encourage you to keep your device protected with an appropriate screen lock and security updates.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Google and its advertising partners may process certain information to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and provide advertising-related services. This may include:

  • Advertising ID or other device identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • App interactions and advertising engagement information.
  • Device, operating system, and technical information.

Advertising data processing is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and advertising settings.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy.

You can manage advertising personalization through your Google account and Android device advertising settings, where available.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information or SMS message contents. We do not share SMS contents or financial transaction records with advertisers.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by the App generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App.

You can manage or remove locally stored records using the available App features or Android application settings.

Certain technical information handled by third-party services may be retained according to those providers' retention policies.

9. Your Choices and Permissions

You have control over the permissions you grant to Quill.

  • You can grant or revoke SMS permissions through Android settings.
  • You can enable or disable notifications through Android settings.
  • You can manage advertising preferences through available Google settings.
  • You can stop using the App and uninstall it at any time.
