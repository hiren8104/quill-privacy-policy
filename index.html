Privacy Policy

App: Quill – Money Manager & SMS Expense Tracker

Developer: Hiren Dabhi

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Quill handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Access

Quill may access SMS messages, with your permission, to identify financial transactions such as payments, deposits, amounts, dates, and transaction descriptions. This helps you track your expenses automatically.

Information you enter manually, such as budgets, categories, and notes, may also be used to provide the app's features.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Identify and organize financial transactions.
  • Display expenses, income, and spending summaries.
  • Help you manage budgets and categories.
  • Maintain and improve app functionality.

3. Advertising

Quill may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising data, and ad interactions to provide and measure advertisements.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use Google services, including Firebase, for features such as app functionality, diagnostics, or notifications. These services may process information as described in their respective privacy policies.

Firebase Privacy Information

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except as needed to provide app features, advertising, diagnostics, or comply with legal requirements.

6. Data Security and Control

You can control app permissions through your device settings. You can revoke SMS permission at any time, although some app features may stop working.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Data handled by third-party services may be subject to their own retention policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Quill is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
